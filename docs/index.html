# Player Pos Team Projected 2025 Age Notes

Projections are season-total fantasy points under half ppr scoring, from a model using prior-season opportunity metrics plus offseason information (team changes, incoming draft capital at the position, coaching changes, new-team offensive context). Week-1 depth charts are excluded by default, since they often publish after August drafts — pass --depth-chart to include them. Rookies are not projected: with no prior NFL season they need a separate model built on draft capital and college production. Kickers and team defenses are not projected either — neither is meaningfully predictable at the season level. Click a column header to sort.
